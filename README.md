# IPL Match Analytics

End-to-end exploratory data analysis on 17 years of IPL data 
covering 1,095 matches and 260,920 deliveries.

## Business Problem
IPL teams, broadcasters, and sponsors make high-stakes decisions 
with limited analytical backing. This analysis answers five key 
business questions using match and ball-by-ball data.

## Dataset
- matches.csv — 1,095 matches, 20 columns
- deliveries.csv — 260,920 deliveries, 17 columns
- Source: Kaggle — IPL Complete Dataset by Prateek Bhardwaj (2008–2024)

## Tools Used
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn

## Key Findings
1. M Theekshana is the most economical death over bowler with an economy of 6.73
2. AB de Villiers is the most reliable death over batter with 223.78 strike rate from 635 balls
3. Chasing teams win more often than batting first — 578 vs 498 matches
4. Wankhede and Eden Gardens produce the most close finishes in IPL history
5. League match batters record higher strike rates than knockout batters, suggesting pressure reduces aggression
